Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Copia is a clipboard manager for macOS. This policy explains what it does with

your data. The short version: **Copia collects nothing, and nothing you copy is

ever sent to us.**

What Copia stores

Copia records what you copy so you can find it again. That history is kept

only on your own Mac, in:

~/Library/Application Support/Copia/

It contains the text, images, and file references you copied, along with the

name of the app you copied them from and the time.

What Copia never records

Copia deliberately ignores clipboard entries marked by the app that created them

as concealed, transient, or auto-generated — the markers password managers use.

Passwords copied from 1Password, Keychain Access, and similar tools are **never

written to history**.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Copia has no servers, no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no

crash reporting, and no advertising. It makes no network connections of its own.

If you switch on iCloud mirroring, Copia copies recent clips into your own

iCloud Drive so you can read them on your iPhone or iPad. That data goes to

your iCloud account, governed by

Apple's privacy policy. We have no access

to it. This setting is off by default, and turning it off deletes the

mirrored copy.

Permissions Copia asks for

PermissionWhy
AccessibilityTo send ⌘V so your chosen item is pasted for you. Copia never records what you type.
Screen RecordingOnly to take the screenshot you asked for with the capture shortcut.

Both are optional. Without them Copia still records history and places items on

your clipboard — you press ⌘V yourself.

Deleting your data

Clear individual items or your whole history from within Copia at any time.

Removing the app and deleting ~/Library/Application Support/Copia/ erases

everything. Since nothing leaves your Mac, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

Copia is not directed at children and collects no personal information from

anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
