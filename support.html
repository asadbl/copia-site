Copia Support

A clipboard manager for macOS. Everything you copy is kept in a searchable

history you can reach from anywhere with a keyboard shortcut.

Get help: open an issue at

github.com/asadbl/copia/issues

Shortcuts

⇧⌘VOpen clipboard history at the pointer
⇧⌘SCapture a screenshot straight to the clipboard
↑ ↓Move through the list
⏎Paste the selected item
⇧⏎Paste without formatting
⌘1–⌘9Paste that row directly
⌘⇧PPin or unpin
⌘⌫Delete from history

escClose

Both shortcuts can be changed in Settings.

Common questions

Copia isn't pasting for me.

It needs Accessibility access: System Settings → Privacy & Security →

Accessibility. If Copia already appears switched on there, **quit and reopen

it** — a permission granted while an app is running doesn't apply until it

restarts.

⇧⌘S does nothing, or asks for permission repeatedly.

Screenshot capture needs Screen Recording access, in the same Privacy & Security

section. It also requires a restart after granting.

Are my passwords stored?

No. Copia ignores clipboard entries that password managers mark as concealed.

Does anything leave my Mac?

No, unless you switch on iCloud mirroring, which copies recent clips to your own

iCloud Drive so you can read them on iPhone. It is off by default.

⇧⌘S conflicts with Save As.

A global shortcut takes precedence over an app's own. Record a different one in

Settings → Screenshots.

How do I stop items expiring?

Settings → History → Keep items for → Forever. Pinned items are never removed

regardless of that setting.
