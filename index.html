Copia

A clipboard manager for macOS

Everything you copy is kept in a searchable history you can reach from anywhere with a keyboard shortcut. Press ⇧⌘V, find what you copied, press Return, and it is pasted into whatever app you were in.

Your history never leaves your Mac. Entries that password managers mark as concealed are never recorded at all.
